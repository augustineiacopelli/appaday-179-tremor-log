A phone cannot measure true Richter magnitude, which describes the energy released at a quake source. Mercalli intensity is the honest translation of local shaking. The magnitude figure is the quake that would shake the ground this hard at the chosen distance, using the Joyner and Boore 1981 attenuation relation, extrapolated below its normal range. Treat it as a feel for scale.
